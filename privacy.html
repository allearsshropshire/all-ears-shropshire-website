Your information matters

Privacy Notice

This notice explains how All Ears Shropshire (trading name of All Ears and More Ltd, referred to as “we”) uses personal information when you contact us or book an appointment.

What we collect

When you call, email, message or book with us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, appointment details and relevant health information needed to assess and treat you safely. We may also record treatment provided and any follow-up advice.

How we use it

  • To arrange, carry out and follow up your appointment.
  • To keep appropriate clinical records.
  • To respond to your questions and enquiries.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

Lawful basis and health information

We use personal information to provide requested services, meet applicable legal obligations and manage our service. Health information receives additional protection under UK data protection law and is used only where an appropriate lawful basis and special-category condition apply.

Online booking

If you book online, your booking is handled by our booking system provider, ManagePatients, which processes booking information in connection with our service. Its own privacy information may also apply when you use its website.

Cookies

We do not intentionally use advertising or analytics tracking cookies on this website. Essential technical cookies or similar technologies may be used by hosting or booking services. Please refer to any information provided by those services when you leave our website.

Keeping your information

We keep personal and clinical records only for as long as necessary for patient care, legal and professional requirements, and legitimate business purposes. The applicable retention period depends on the type of record and relevant requirements. You can contact us to ask about retention of your information.

Sharing your information

We share information only where needed to provide the service, with providers supporting our booking or administration, when you request or authorise a referral, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect someone’s safety.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, or object to certain uses. Some information, including clinical records, may need to be retained despite a deletion request. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
