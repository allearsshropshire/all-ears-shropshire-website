Our services

Gentle, professional ear care

Your ears are unique. The most suitable approach will be discussed following an ear-health assessment.

01 / MICROSUCTION

Microsuction

A controlled method of removing ear wax using gentle suction under direct visual guidance, where clinically appropriate.

02 / IRRIGATION

Ear Irrigation

Carefully controlled water irrigation may be suitable for some patients following screening and assessment.

03 / MANUAL REMOVAL

Manual Removal

Specialist instruments can be used to remove accessible wax where this is considered appropriate and safe.
