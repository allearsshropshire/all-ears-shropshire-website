Booking & Cancellation Policy

Helpful information before your home visit

Booking an appointment

Appointments are subject to availability and confirmation. Please tell us about previous ear surgery, ear infections, ear problems or other relevant health information when booking, so we can assess whether treatment may be suitable.

Changing or cancelling

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible on 07737 993301 or allearsshropshire@gmail.com.

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or rearrange an appointment. A £25 fee applies to cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice or missed appointments. If something unexpected happens, please contact us as soon as possible.

Assessment and suitability

We assess your ears before recommending any wax removal. Treatment is provided only if suitable following screening and assessment. If treatment is not appropriate, we will explain the findings and discuss next steps, which may include seeking advice from your GP or another appropriate healthcare professional.

When no wax is found

If no wax requiring removal is found at a wax-removal appointment, a reduced £30 assessment fee applies. A separately booked Ear Health Check costs £35.

Follow-up care

If wax cannot be fully removed at the first appointment, we offer a free follow-up where a further attempt is clinically appropriate. This does not guarantee that all wax can be removed.

Home visits and travel

Travel within the advertised local area is included in standard prices. Visits outside that area may incur an additional charge, confirmed before your appointment.

Questions?

Call 07737 993301 or email allearsshropshire@gmail.com. See our frequently asked questions for more information.
